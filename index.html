GODIA PRIME AI Agent

Your built-in assistant for marketplace and business operations.

PROTECTED AI

AI operating policy

The agent is allowed to help with marketplace operations, but sensitive owner actions are outside its scope.

✓ Allowed
  • Search posted goods
  • Read public supplier listings
  • Summarize orders and stock signals
  • Suggest reorder quantities
  • Help users choose categories
  • Draft order notes
  • Flag incomplete listings
✕ Denied
  • Private AI keys
  • Payment approval or alteration
  • Private customer-data export
  • Deleting records
  • Owner settings or PIN changes
  • Publishing without supplier action
  • Moving money or making purchases

Ask the agent

I can help find posted goods, inspect marketplace information, summarize orders and identify missing listing information. I cannot approve payments, expose secrets or change protected settings.
